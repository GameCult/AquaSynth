Nothing here was heard. These are metrics from offline renders (faustwasm, mono, 48 kHz, 3 s per patch); they are evidence,
not verdicts. `tools/aqua-feel/README.md` says what each number is and where it breaks.

loudness; sfxr golf: true peak, loudness, low motion; sfxr explosion: low motion). After: PRC SYNTH1 gain trimmed 1.4 to
0.45 (a uniform gain: crest and centroid are unchanged), and `thruster.dsp` mix edited (below). Gate after: pass, 4 waivers
(golf x3, explosion x1), 1 warning (golf, +0.9 dBTP).

## thruster

Edits to `patches/thruster/thruster.dsp` (mirrored in `tools/aqua-trial/thruster-trial.dsp`): rasp gain `0.3 + 1.5*load` to
`0.05 + 1.75*load`; rattle floor `0.1 + 0.9*load^2` to `0.015 + 0.985*load^2`; rumble low-pass `60 + 2.5*shaft` to
`60 + 2.5*shaft + 140*(1-load)`. At load 1 (rpm 6000) every term equals its old value (rasp 1.8, rattle 1, low-pass 60 + 2.5*shaft).
Decomposition at rpm 300 before the edit (A-weighted noise-to-tone, dB): full mix +21, rasp removed +20.5, rattle removed
+13.0, both removed +12.5. The rattle modes, not the rasp, were most of the audible-band noise at low rpm; with both gone a
floor of +12.5 dB remains (the pitch-wandering saw smears its harmonics, which the line detector counts as noise).

The centroid falls a lot at low rpm: the sound is darker as well as less noisy. Whether that is "more tone" to a listener or

rpm 600 (see its README).
